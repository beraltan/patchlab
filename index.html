Twin rear dovetails with a printed snap key. No glue or hardware. Adds 8.5 mm behind the face plate. Print a joint coupon to check fit first.

Ready-to-print test (.stl) ↗

The STL test set is 156 x 90 mm with 3 mm sample faces. Test fits: 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30 mm per surface. Each key has its clearance engraved on the back. Pick the tightest fit that slides fully and latches without forcing, then enter that value above. Use your final material, layer height and orientation.

Ribs stiffen the back while leaving the connector mounting face at its set thickness. Rack ears have uniform full-depth backing; use solid infill in these areas and rack screws long enough for the thicker ears. D-series starts with a 3 mm mounting limit; verify your exact model. Keep space for rear connector bodies and tools.

FROM PIXELS TO PLASTIC

Ready for your workbench.

Download STL files for your slicer, straight from the browser.

For two-color printing, import each section's body and labels together as parts of one object. Keep their shared coordinates and assign a filament to each.

Or export OpenSCAD and render it yourself
  1. Open the file in OpenSCAD. Set part = "body", render (F6), and export STL.
  2. Set part = "labels", render, and export a second STL.
  3. Import both STLs together as one object with multiple parts. Keep their shared coordinates and assign your two filaments.

With ribs or split joints enabled, exported sections are face-down: lettering occupies the first inlay layers. A plain, unsplit export keeps the original face-up orientation. The browser preview uses an approximate font; inspect the rendered lettering before printing.

Joining key and fit guide

Bring the panel sections together. Slide the key into both rear channels from the lower edge. The integral tongue retains the key; no glue, nuts or bolts are needed for the panel joint. Connector mounting hardware is still model-specific.
